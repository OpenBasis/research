# T11. Symbolic capital bridge

With N = matched short notional, R = reserve, C = total capital:

- funding contribution = (N/C) x sum(funding)
- reserve contribution = (R/C) x sum(reserve accrual)
- cost contribution = cost dollars / C

No value of N/C or R/C is selected; no weights are chosen. A yield-bearing
reserve is not risk-free cash: calling its return a gain or a drag requires
an opportunity-cost comparator that Study 0 does not select. Capital placed
in reserve dilutes funding capture per unit of capital and is subject to
delayed availability. Unlike return conventions are never summed; the
funding sum and the reserve accrual sum remain separate quantities.
